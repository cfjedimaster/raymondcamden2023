I've been writing here for a long time about the web platform, APIs, AI demos, tooling, and generally building things. If you've got something that would actually be useful to that crowd, I'm open to talking about sponsorship.

This page is a starting point, not a media kit. Details like placement and packages are still being figured out. If it sounds like a fit, email me and we'll talk.

## Who shows up

These are approximate numbers from Netlify Analytics for a recent 30-day stretch (about August 9 through September 8, 2026). They're a snapshot, not a promise:

* Around 536k pageviews
* Around 82k unique visitors

What tends to do well: builder demos, my [Links For You](/tags/links4you/) roundups, and evergreen how-tos that people keep finding years later.

## What this can look like

I'm flexible. Nothing here is a hard sell, and rates are by request — just email me.

A few things that might make sense:

* A mention in a Links For You post
* An honest, hands-on demo or review when the product is a genuine fit
* Site placement, still TBD

I'll only write about something if I can be honest about it. I won't fake enthusiasm. If I try it and I don't like it, I'd rather say so (or pass) than pretend.

## What I won't do

* Anything that would break trust with readers
* Icky SEO stuff, link schemes, or "guest posts" that exist only to rank
* Products I wouldn't use myself or wouldn't recommend to a friend

If that sounds strict, good. That's the point.
