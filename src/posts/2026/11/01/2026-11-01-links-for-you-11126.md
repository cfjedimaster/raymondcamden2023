## When job hunting turns into a security lesson

Everyone knows the job market is hell now, and to be honest, hell may be a bit too kind. Now imagine you are considering a job, download a take home test, and discover it's actually a hack attempt. Appaji C. details the attack here: ["I Inspected My Take-Home Interview Project. It Was a Whole Operation."](https://citizendot.github.io/articles/fake-job-interview-git-hook-malware/?utm_source=tldrdev)

Of special note, I love the Halloween theme on his blog. It may be gone by the time you read this, but it's gorgeous.

## There's an app for that - but there shouldn't be

Next up is a fun little post (and rant) from Dan Q. titled, ["Your 'App' Could Have Been a Webpage (so I fixed it for you...)"](https://danq.me/2026/07/09/your-app-could-have-been-a-webpage/?utm_source=the-index&utm_medium=newsletter). Now, this may be hard to believe, but Dan was asked to download an app that was absolutely clearly something that should have been just a web page. Shocker, right? But instead of just complaining about it, Dan went to work to inspect the app and tear out the important information. This then turned into a script he could use to generate his own HTML. More of this, please. 

## Modernist tiles with CSS Grid

I've been saying this now for maybe 10 years, but CSS has moved at light speed to the point where *anything* seems possible. This is great for everyone. Carmen Ansio created a beautiful tutorial on recreating a modernist tile look using CSS: ["Modernist tiles with CSS Grid"](https://www.carmenansio.com/articles/modernist-tiles-css-grid/). The post goes into detail about the methods used, and has multiple interactive elements that are really well done. 

Credit for this find comes from the excellent [newsletter](https://cassidoo.co/newsletter/) by Cassidy Williams.
