## Make Music, Not War

First up is the appropriately named [Hyperblam](https://hyperblam.how/), a music making tool that lets you use a declarative language to create sick beats as the kids say. What does that look like? Here's one example:

```html
<!-- bus! (master signal chain) -->
<chain-blam id="bus">
<gain-blam id="bits" gain="0.25" bypass>
    <lfo-blam type="square" beats="0.001" prop="gain" gain="0.25">
    <blam-blam from="#kick" prop="beats" value="0.001~0.003" chance="0.3"></blam-blam>
    </lfo-blam>
</gain-blam>
<phaser-blam id="swoosh" center="6000" depth="4000" q="0.5" bypass></phaser-blam>
<analyser-blam id="analyser" smoothing="0.6"></analyser-blam>
</chain-blam>
```

## Your new RSS Feed Reader

Last but definitely not least is an incredible new product from my buddy [Robert](https://kisdigital.com/) - [Feedhouse](https://feedhouse.app/) is an RSS reader, but with so many features it goes far beyond just RSS parsing. It has great social aspects (you can mark up and comment on items in your feed, you can share collections of feeds), it supports sites without RSS feeds, it lets you sign up for newsletters with a custom address and automatically parse the items into your feed, and so much more. If you want to learn more about how it's built, Robert wrote up a great post on it: [Everything Feedhouse Does, From the Boring Parts Up](https://kisdigital.com/posts/2026/10/everything-feedhouse-does-from-the-boring-parts-up).
